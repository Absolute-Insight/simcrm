# Weekly planning

The Planner is a week at a time: what you intend to do, resolved against what
you actually did. It is reached from **Planner** in the sidebar.

## Making a plan

Add items by hand, or press **Propose my week**. That drafts a week from your
ten highest-scoring open suggestions, spread across the working days, each one
carrying the activity it implies — a *schedule call* suggestion becomes a call,
the rest become tasks.

Proposing writes nothing. You get a draft in the grid; edit it, delete what you
disagree with, and save. Nothing is committed until you do.

## Plan against actual

Once a day, planned items are matched against real logged activity — a call
log, a task, a communication on the record you planned it against. A match
marks the item **Done** and records what fulfilled it, so the link is auditable
rather than a claim.

Matching is oldest-week-first, so an activity is claimed by the week it was
planned for rather than by a later week it merely overlaps. Nothing is matched
twice.

An item past its date with nothing to match becomes **Missed**, and a missed
item is itself a signal — it will come back to you in the inbox.

You can also mark an item done or missed yourself, and clear that override
again if you were wrong.

## Reading it back

**Plan adherence by rep** in Reports is the aggregate: planned, done, missed,
and the percentage. A manager sees the reps below them in the sales hierarchy.

The point of the pair is that a plan nobody checks is a wish. This one checks
itself.
