# Vectora on a phone

Vectora is a progressive web app, so there is nothing to install from a store.

**Open the CRM in your phone's browser, then add it to your home screen** —
*Share → Add to Home Screen* on iOS, *Install app* or *Add to Home screen* in
Chrome on Android. It then opens full-screen, without browser chrome, and
behaves like an app.

## What is different on a phone

The layout adapts rather than shrinking. Two things that are slide-over panels
on a desktop become pages of their own, because there is nowhere for a panel to
live on a 375px screen:

- **Suggestions**
- **Notifications**

The reports rail becomes a picker above the table, and so does this help
centre's article list.

## Notifications

Realtime updates — a new suggestion, an assignment — arrive while the app is
open. Push notifications when it is closed depend on your browser and platform
and are not something Vectora controls.
