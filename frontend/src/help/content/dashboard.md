# The dashboard

The dashboard is role-aware, and that is deliberate: a rep opening it wants
"what needs me today", a manager wants "how is the team doing". Those are
different pages, so they are two different sets of panels chosen by role rather
than a widget picker every rep would have to assemble for themselves.

## Stat tiles

Across the top: **Leads** (team view only), **Open deals**, **Won deals**,
**Plan adherence** and **Quota attainment**, each with its change against the
previous period. A tile with no change to show does not show one — a delta of
zero is noise.

Tiles drill in. Clicking one takes you to the list it counts, already filtered.

## Panels

**Needs your attention** appears for everyone: the deals a signal is firing on,
ranked by urgency with the reason attached. It draws on the same signals as the
inbox, narrowed to the four that describe a deal in trouble rather than the ones
that merely propose work.

**Today**, for a rep: your plan for today against what is already done.

**Plan adherence by rep** and **Quota attainment**, for a manager: one row per
rep in your part of the sales hierarchy, with anything below 60% adherence or
80% attainment marked.

You can hide panels you do not use; the choice is remembered in the browser.

## Filters

Period, territory, and — for a manager — a specific rep. Reading one rep's
dashboard shows you that rep's numbers throughout, panels and charts alike, not
a mixture.

## Charts

Below the panels, managers get the customisable chart grid: add, arrange and
save a layout. It is kept rather than replaced because it is the only place
charts live and managers already have layouts saved in it. **Reset to Default**
puts it back.

Charts group by things that are sometimes not set — a deal with no territory,
a deal with no owner. Those rows are labelled **Unassigned** rather than
dropped, because a chart that silently omits them tells you a smaller number
than the truth.
