# Email

## Connecting an account

**Settings → Email → Accounts.** Add the account the team sends from. Most
providers need an app password rather than your ordinary one — the setup form
links to the right page for the common providers as you pick them.

This matters more than it looks. Without a connected account the CRM cannot see
a conversation at all, and several things stop working quietly rather than
loudly:

- the thread on a record is empty,
- the **idle deal** and **deal cooling** signals have nothing to read, because a
  communication is one of the four things that count as activity,
- the assistant has no thread to summarise or reply to.

## Templates

**Settings → Email → Templates.** Reusable bodies with the record's fields
substituted in, so the same first-touch email is not retyped forty times.

## Sending from a record

The communication area on a lead or deal sends, and what you send is logged
against the record as activity — which is what keeps the signals honest. An
email sent from your own client, outside the CRM, is invisible to it, and the
deal will look idle because as far as Vectora can tell, it is.
