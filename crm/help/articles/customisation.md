Vectora is customisable per site without touching code. Most of this lives in
**Settings** and needs an admin role.

## Fields and layouts

- **Custom fields** — add fields to leads, deals, contacts and organizations.
- **Quick entry layout** — choose which fields the create dialog asks for.
- **Form layout** — arrange the fields on record pages into tabs, sections
  and columns (**Settings → Forms**).

## Statuses and pipelines

- Lead and deal **statuses are records, not code** — rename, recolour,
  reorder, or add your own stages to match how your team actually sells.

## Actions

- **Home Actions** (**Settings → Home Actions**) customise the quick actions
  on the home screen.
- **Custom actions** can add buttons to record pages.

## Form scripts (for developers)

Admins with development skills can attach **Form Script** records to a
doctype: JavaScript that runs on the record page and can show/hide fields,
set properties, validate, or open dialogs through a stable scripting API.
Scripts are additive and versioned with your site, not with the product.

## Branding

- **Settings → Brand** — logo and product name shown in the sidebar and
  login screen.
