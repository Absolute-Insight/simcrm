A target is one number: what a rep should close in a month. **Settings → Sales
Targets**, laid out as a grid of reps down and months across, for one year at a
time.

Only a manager can set them.

## Setting them

Type a figure into a cell and it saves. **Copy forward** takes one month's
figure and applies it to the rest of the year, which is the usual case — a
target that changes every month is the exception, not the norm.

A rep with no target set is not shown as 0% attainment; they are shown as having
no target. Those are different facts and the reports keep them apart.

## Where a target shows up

- **Quota attainment** stat tile on the dashboard.
- **Quota attainment** panel on a manager's dashboard, one row per rep, with
  anything under 80% marked.
- **Quota attainment by rep** in Reports, with the gap in currency as well as
  the percentage.

Attainment is closed-won revenue against the target — realised value, not
expected. A large forecast does not move it. See **Forecasting and sales
targets** for how the two relate.
