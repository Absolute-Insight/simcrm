A lead is an unqualified prospect: a person or company that has shown interest
but is not yet a deal.

## Capturing leads

- **Create manually** with the + button on the Leads list.
- **Web forms** — publish a form and submissions arrive as leads.
- **Import** — Settings-driven bulk import from CSV.
- **Lead syncing** — pull leads in from connected sources
  (**Settings → Lead Syncing**).

## Working a lead

- The lead page shows the record's fields on the left and its full timeline —
  emails, calls, comments, tasks and notes — in the middle.
- **Status** tracks where the lead is. Statuses are customisable
  per site, so your list may differ from the default.
- **Assignment** — assign a lead to a rep by hand, or let
  **Assignment Rules** route new leads automatically.
- **Response SLA** — if an SLA policy is active, the lead shows its response
  target, and an untouched lead past the target raises a *lead SLA* suggestion
  for its owner.

## Converting to a deal

When a lead is qualified, use **Convert to Deal**. Conversion creates the deal
and carries over the contact and organization; the timeline moves with it, so
nothing said before qualification is lost.

## Tips

- Filters, sorting, grouping and column layouts are all saveable as **views** —
  see **Views, filters and saved lists**.
- The kanban view groups leads by status and supports drag-and-drop between
  columns.
