The dashboard is role-aware: it answers *what needs me today* for a rep and
*how is the team tracking* for a manager. Both read the same tested metrics
layer as the reports — one source of numbers, so two screens never disagree.

## For reps

- **Stat tiles** — your open deals, won deals, plan adherence, quota
  attainment and critical deals for the selected period.
- **Needs your attention** — the most urgent suggestions, each with its
  reason, ranked exactly as in the inbox.
- **Today** — your plan for today against what is already done.

## For managers

Everything above, plus:

- **Team tiles and filters** — filter the whole page by period, by rep, or by
  territory. Charts that cannot honour the territory filter say so on the tile
  rather than silently showing global numbers.
- **Plan adherence by rep** and **Quota attainment** panels — the same rows
  the corresponding reports show.
- **The chart grid** — a customisable grid of charts (sales trend, funnel
  conversion, deals by stage / source / territory / industry, forecast,
  forecast accuracy and more). Press **Edit** to rearrange or resize, **Chart**
  to add one, and **Reset to Default** to restore the stock layout.

## Reading it

- **Click a tile** to open the list of the exact records behind the number —
  the dashboard is meant to be worked from, not just read.
- **Deltas** compare against the previous period of the same length. For
  metrics where lower is better (time to close, critical deals), a falling
  number shows as improvement. A tile with no change does not show one.
- Rows below 60% adherence or 80% attainment are marked in the manager
  panels.
- Charts group by things that are sometimes not set — a deal with no
  territory or no owner. Those rows are labelled **Unassigned** rather than
  dropped.
- **Panels** can be hidden or reordered with the controls in each panel's
  corner; hidden panels stay one click away at the bottom of the page.

## Choosing the period

The period selector offers the usual presets (last 7/30/60/90 days) and a
custom range. Quota attainment pro-rates the target by covered days, so a
one-week range compares a week of revenue against a week's worth of target.
