Search opens from anywhere, so you never need to go to a list and filter it just
to find one record.

## Opening it

- Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or click **Search** at the top of the
  sidebar.
- On a phone, tap the magnifying glass in the top bar.

## What it finds

Type two or more characters to search **leads, deals, contacts and
organizations**. You can search by:

- a name, company, email address or phone number
- a **quote number**, such as QT103406, which finds the deal
- an **Acumatica customer ID**, such as C-LON001J, which finds the customer

A deal shows its quote number beside it, because all of a customer's deals carry
the customer's name.

You only ever see records you are allowed to open. Search and the lists follow
the same rules, and a page your role does not have stays out of the results too.

## With nothing typed

- **Upcoming** lists what you planned for today and tomorrow, then your tasks due
  soon. A task more than three days overdue drops off this list, but stays on your
  Tasks page.
- **Recent** lists the records you opened last.

## Getting around

Search also goes to any page you have in the sidebar, and creates a lead, deal,
task or note. Type "planner" or "create lead", then press **Enter**. A new lead or
deal opens straight away, wherever you started from.

Use the arrow keys to move and **Esc** to close.
