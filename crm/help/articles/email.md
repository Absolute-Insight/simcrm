Email lives on the record it belongs to: the full thread with a lead or deal
is on that record's timeline, not in a separate inbox.

## Sending

- Open a lead or deal and compose from the timeline. Replies keep the thread.
- **Templates** (**Settings → Email → Templates**) pre-fill common replies;
  pick one from the compose window.
- Attachments added to a message are also kept on the record.

## Receiving

- Mail to a connected account (**Settings → Email → Accounts**) is matched to
  the record by the sender's address and appears on the timeline. Most
  providers need an app password rather than your ordinary one.
- Without a connected account the thread on a record is empty, the *idle* and
  *cooling* signals have no communications to read, and the assistant has
  nothing to summarise. Mail sent from your own client, outside Vectora, is
  invisible to it — the deal will look idle because, as far as Vectora can
  tell, it is.

## Summaries and drafted replies

With the assistant's model tier enabled (**Settings → Assistant**):

- **Summarise thread** condenses a long thread into a summary, suggested next
  steps and an overall sentiment.
- **Draft reply** proposes a reply to the latest inbound message. The draft
  opens in the compose window for you to edit — Vectora never sends anything
  itself.

Treat both as a first pass, not a verdict: they are generated from the thread,
and the thread contains whatever the other party chose to write. Read before
you trust, and always before you send. See **The Vectora assistant**.
