Contacts are people; organizations are the companies they work for. Deals link
to both.

## Contacts

- A contact holds emails, phone numbers, and the organization they belong to.
- The contact page lists every deal and lead the person is involved in.
- Emails to and from a contact's address appear on the timeline of the linked
  records automatically.

## Organizations

- An organization holds the company-level facts: website, industry, size,
  territory, annual revenue.
- The organization page lists all its contacts and deals, so account context
  is one click away from any deal.
- **Enrich from website** — Vectora can read a company's public website and
  propose values for empty fields (industry, description). Proposed values are
  shown for review before anything is written.

## Territories

Organizations and deals can carry a **territory**. Managers can filter the
dashboard by territory; the deals-by-territory chart breaks revenue down by it.

## Tips

- Keep one organization per company and link contacts to it, rather than
  duplicating the company name on each contact — the account view and the
  by-organization analytics depend on the link.

## Whose customer it is

Every organization can have an **Account Owner**, the rep whose customer it is.

- A rep sees the customers they own, customers nobody owns yet, and customers they
  have a deal or a planned visit with. Other reps' customers, and the contacts,
  addresses and events at them, stay out of their lists and search.
- A rep can open a deal or plan a visit only on a customer they can see. Their first
  deal on an unowned customer, or a customer they add, makes it theirs.
- Only a manager can change the Account Owner, on the customer's page.

An administrator can switch this off under **Settings → Access Control**.
