The Mentor is the sparkle next to the Help Center title. Click it and type a
question about Vectora in your own words — "how does plan adherence get
counted?", "where do I set targets?", "why did this deal get flagged?" — and
it answers from these help articles, showing the ones it drew on as chips
under the answer. Click a chip to open that article; the conversation is kept,
and **Back to conversation** returns you to it.

## What it does for you

- **Answers questions about how Vectora works** — features, settings, how a
  number is computed, where something lives — without you having to find the
  right article first.
- **Cites its sources.** Every answer lists the articles it used, and only
  articles that really exist in this help center can appear there, so a chip
  is never a dead end.
- **Says when the manual does not cover something**, rather than inventing an
  answer. If you get that reply, the search field and the category tree are
  the next step.

## When it runs

Only when you ask. Nothing happens in the background, and a question is
answered in the moment and then forgotten — the conversation lives in your
browser tab and is gone when you close it.

It is available to every user once an administrator has enabled the model in
**Settings → Assistant** (see **The Assistant**, *Enabling the model*). Until
then the box says the Mentor is off, and the rest of the help center works as
usual.

## What it never does

- **It never reads your records.** It cannot see deals, leads, contacts,
  email or your settings. Its whole knowledge is this help center, so it can
  tell you how Vectora treats a pipeline, never anything about *your*
  pipeline. "Why did *this* deal get flagged?" is answered by the deal page's
  **Needs attention** section; the Mentor can only explain what the factors
  mean.
- **It never changes anything.** It cannot create, edit or delete records,
  send email or alter settings.
- **It never answers from outside the manual.** A question about your
  company's products belongs to **The Assistant**; a question about your
  numbers belongs to **The Analyst**.

## Mentor, Assistant or Analyst?

| You want to know | Ask |
|---|---|
| How a Vectora feature works, where a setting is | **The Mentor**, here in the help center |
| What your company sells, what it is rated for, who uses it | **The Assistant**, in the sidebar |
| How the business is doing, from the figures | **The Analyst**, in the sidebar — administrators only |

All three read a model's answer the same way: as a proposal to check. See
**What Vectora does for you automatically**, *How to read a model's answer*.
