Every list in Vectora — leads, deals, contacts, tasks, calls — shares the same
controls: filters, sorting, grouping, column layout, and three view shapes
(list, kanban, group-by). Learn them once, use them everywhere.

## Filters and sorting

- **Filter** builds conditions on any field, combined with and/or.
- **Sort** orders by one or more fields.
- **Columns** picks and reorders the visible columns; widths are draggable.

## Saved views

Arrange a list the way you want and save it as a **view**:

- **Saved views** are yours, listed in the sidebar under the module.
- **Public views** are shared with everyone on the site.
- **Pinned views** stay at the top of the sidebar for one-click access.

Dashboard drill-downs write the same kind of view the lists read, so clicking
a tile lands you in a list you can immediately refine and save.

## Kanban

The kanban view groups by status (or another select field) with drag-and-drop
between columns — moving a card updates the record.

## Quick filters

The filter row above a list offers the most-used fields for that module so
common narrowing is one click, no filter builder needed.
