Everything below lives in **Settings** (the gear in the sidebar dropdown).
You need a manager or administrator role for most of it.

## Users and roles

- **Settings → Users** — see everyone with access and their role.
- **Settings → Invite User** — invite teammates by email.
- Roles: **Sales User** works their own records, **Sales Manager** sees the
  team's, **Admin** configures the site.
- **Settings → Sales Hierarchy** — define who reports to whom. The hierarchy
  scopes what managers see everywhere: deal lists, dashboards, plans and
  reports all follow it.

## Email

- **Settings → Email → Accounts** — connect the mailboxes Vectora sends and
  receives through.
- **Settings → Email → Templates** — reusable templates for common replies.

## Telephony and messaging

- **Settings → Telephony** — connect Twilio or Exotel to call from inside
  Vectora and log calls automatically.
- **Settings → WhatsApp** — connect WhatsApp Business messaging.

## Targets and forecasting

- **Settings → Sales Targets** — monthly targets per rep, in the base currency.
  Quota attainment on the dashboard and in reports is measured against these.

## Automation and the assistant

- **Settings → Assignment Rules** — route incoming leads to the right rep.
- **Settings → SLA Policies** — response-time targets for new leads.
- **Settings → Automation Rules** — deterministic if-this-then-that rules.
- **Settings → Assistant** — enable the optional model tier (thread summaries,
  reply drafts, the chat assistant) by pointing it at your own inference
  endpoint. See **What Vectora does for you automatically** for what this does and does not do.

## Branding

- **Settings → Brand** — your logo and name, shown across the app.
