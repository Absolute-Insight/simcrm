Activity is what plan-vs-actual, adherence and the signals all measure, so
logging it is not busywork — it is what makes the proactive features accurate.

## Tasks

- Add a task from any lead or deal, with a due date and priority.
- An open deal with **no open task** raises a *no next step* suggestion: every
  active deal should know its next move.
- Marking a task **Done** is what fulfils a planned *Task* item in the planner.

## Notes and comments

- **Notes** are longer-form records attached to a lead or deal.
- **Comments** are quick timeline entries, with @-mentions to pull a teammate
  in. Mentioned users are notified.

## Calls

- With telephony connected (**Settings → Telephony**), call from the record
  page and the call log writes itself, including duration and recording where
  available.
- Call logs count as activity for the idle-deal signal and fulfil planned
  *Call* items — matched on caller **or** receiver, so calls logged by the
  telephony integration still credit the rep who made them.

## Meetings

- Calendar events linked to a record count as meetings. A completed,
  non-cancelled event fulfils a planned *Meeting* item.
- The **Calendar** page shows your schedule across all records.
